Core installation

Full kitchen extraction system install — supply, fabrication, electrical and commissioning, carried out entirely by our own engineers. Everything below is included in the price unless stated under Exclusions.

Extraction System

Stainless steel extraction canopy hoodSized to cover the cooking line, grease baffles included
£8,400

304-grade stainless steel canopy, sized and positioned to cover the full cooking line confirmed at survey. 304-grade is the commercial kitchen standard — corrosion resistant, easy to clean, built to last. Grease baffles fitted as standard.

High-pressure centrifugal fan with inverter driveSpecified to the duct run's static pressure
Included

Fan selected to overcome the static pressure resistance of the surveyed duct run. The inverter drive gives variable speed control — turn it down during quieter periods to cut noise and energy use, up to full extraction during service.

In-line acoustic silencerFitted between fan and discharge point
Included

Attenuates fan noise at source. In a live trading environment, noise bleed from kitchen extraction into customer-facing space is a real concern — the silencer is specified to address this rather than relying on duct lagging alone.

Ductwork & Distribution

Ductwork fabrication & installFull run from canopy to discharge point
Included

All ductwork fabricated in-house to the route confirmed on site survey — including all bends, transitions and sections to complete the run. Exact length confirmed on installation day; the price reflects the surveyed estimate.

TR19-compliant access panelsInstalled at 2-metre intervals throughout the run
Included

A mandatory TR19 requirement — access panels must be present throughout the duct run for inspection and cleaning. Without them, the system cannot receive a TR19 certificate and your insurance may be at risk.

Rubber flexible connectionsAt canopy spigot and fan inlet/outlet
Included

Isolates vibration from the fan motor so it doesn't transmit through the ductwork into the building structure, and allows minor positional tolerance during installation.
